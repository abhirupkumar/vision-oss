---
title: Join an organization
---

To join an organization, you will need the owner to send you an invitation email.

### Join via email

1. Click on the invite link in your email.
2. If you already have a Vision account, log in.
3. If you do not have an account, you will be prompted to create one.
4. After logging in, you will be added to the organization automatically.

You will now be able to see the organization listed under your **Organizations** page.
