---
title: Add an organization
---

To add an organization, navigate to **Organizations** and click on the **Create organization** button.

Fill out the form and click **Save**.

## Add a website

Under **Websites**, click on the **Add website** button. Fill out the form details and click **Save**. See [Add a website](/docs/add-a-website) for more details.

## Invite members

Under **Members**, click on the **Invite member** button. Fill out the form details and click **Save**. Wait for the user to accept the invite via email.
