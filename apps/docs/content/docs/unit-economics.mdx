title: Unit Economics & Metric Definitions
---

In AI applications, software margins are directly impacted by continuous model inference costs. Vision provides automated unit economics monitoring by reconciling payment data with token compute telemetry.

## Key Financial Formulas

### 1. Cost-to-Serve (CTS)
Cost-to-Serve measures the total inference and infrastructure compute cost incurred to support an active user or feature interaction:

```text
CTS = Sum(Prompt Tokens × Input Price + Completion Tokens × Output Price)
```

Tracking CTS allows finance and engineering teams to identify which customer workflows or templates are driving model expenses.

### 2. Gross Margin per User
Gross Margin per User represents the net contribution of each customer account after deducting their direct model compute cost:

```text
Gross Margin (User) = Attributed Revenue - Inference Cost
```

Accounts with negative gross margins indicate that heavy prompt usage exceeds their subscription billing tier.

### 3. Blended Gross Margin Percentage
The overall financial health of your AI platform:

```text
Gross Margin % = ((Total Attributed Revenue - Total AI Inference Cost) / Total Attributed Revenue) × 100
```

Sustainable generative AI SaaS products typically target a gross margin of **70% to 85%**.

### 4. AI ROI Ratio
Measures revenue generated relative to model inference investment:

```text
AI ROI = (Net Expansion + New MRR Driven by AI Features) / Total Model Compute Cost
```

A ratio of **5x or higher** demonstrates strong commercial return on model spending.

### 5. Inference Payback Velocity
Calculates how many months of customer gross margin are needed to amortize customer acquisition cost (CAC):

```text
Payback Months = CAC / (Monthly Revenue per User - Monthly CTS)
```

## Margin Health Distribution

Vision automatically classifies accounts into 4 margin tiers:

| Tier | Gross Margin | Operational Action |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Unprofitable** | Negative (&lt; 0%) | Model cost exceeds revenue. Trigger rate limiting, model downgrades, or quota alerts. |
| **At Risk** | 0% - 50% | Low margin. Investigate prompt caching or template optimization. |
| **Healthy** | 50% - 80% | Sustainable standard software margin. |
| **High Margin** | Above 80% | Exceptional unit economics. Candidates for account expansion and upsell. |
