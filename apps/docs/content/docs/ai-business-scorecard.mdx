The **AI Business Scorecard** is an executive dashboard designed for founders, finance leaders, and product engineering teams to observe product adoption, inference infrastructure costs, and commercial profitability on a single canvas.

## Key Scorecard Widgets

### 1. Blended Gross Margin
* Displays net revenue after subtracting continuous LLM model costs.
* Shows trend comparisons against the previous period (e.g., WoW or MoM).
* Signals margin compression when token volume expands faster than customer plan tiers.

### 2. Cost-to-Serve (CTS) Telemetry
* Computes average model inference expense per daily active user (DAU).
* Benchmarks cost-to-serve against SaaS industry thresholds (target: under 10% of ARPU).

### 3. AI ROI Ratio
* Measures new revenue and customer expansions generated per dollar spent on model compute.
* Evaluates feature economic viability before scaling enterprise rollouts.

### 4. Accounts at Loss Warning
* Flags customers whose prompt executions exceed their subscription price.
* Provides direct drill-downs into token consumption and model distribution for remediation.

## Executive Setup Checklist

1. **Connect Payments**: Enable Stripe, Paddle, Polar, or Lemon Squeezy in **Settings -> Billing**.
2. **Instrument User IDs**: Pass `X-Vision-User-Id` on all model proxy calls.
3. **Configure Guardrails**: Set budget alert thresholds in **Unit Economics -> Budget Guardrails** to notify Slack or Discord if a customer exceeds spend quotas.
4. **Link Team Members**: Invite finance and product leaders to the Vision workspace with viewer or admin permissions.
