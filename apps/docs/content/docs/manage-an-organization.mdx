---
title: Manage an organization
---

Depending on your role, you can edit the organization settings and manage members.

Navigate to **Organizations** and click on the **Edit** button next to the organization. 
Alternatively, click on the **Settings** tab within the organization's dashboard.

![image](/images/docs/team-details.png)

## Details

You can change the organization name under the **Details** tab.

## Members

To invite a new member to your organization, click on the **Invite member** button under the **Members** tab. 

When you invite a member, you must assign them a role:
- **Organization Owner**
- **Organization Manager** 
- **Organization Member**
- **View Only**

## Websites

To add a website to the organization, click on the **Add website** button under the **Websites** tab.

All organization members will automatically have access to view analytics for the added websites based on their organization role.
