---
title: Business Insights & Observability
---

Vision provides operational and financial insights to give operators, product teams, and finance leaders complete visibility into their software business.

Together, these insights bridge user interaction, model performance, and financial margins.

## Available Insights

### 💰 Unit Economics & Financials
- [Unit Economics](/docs/unit-economics) — Monitor gross margin per user, Cost-to-Serve (CTS), and AI ROI ratios.
- [AI Business Scorecard](/docs/ai-business-scorecard) — Executive dashboard monitoring revenue, inference burn, and net profitability.
- [Revenue by User](/docs/revenue-by-user) — Track per-customer subscriptions, payment history, and compute margins.
- [Finance Attribution](/docs/finance-attribution) — Multi-tier identity hierarchy linking marketing touchpoints to recurring revenue.

### 🤖 AI Observability
- [LLM Observability](/docs/llm-observability) — Full prompt traces, token volume, streaming TTFT latency, and model efficiency benchmarks.
- [MCP Server Guide](/docs/guides/setup-mcp-server) — Query your live metrics and scorecards from Claude Desktop, Cursor, or AI agents using the Model Context Protocol.

### 📊 Product Analytics & Behavior
- [Funnel](/docs/funnel) — Measure conversion velocity and drop-off rates across product flows.
- [Journey](/docs/journey) — Visualize user navigation paths and multi-step drop-offs with Sankey diagrams.
- [Retention](/docs/retention) — Track weekly and monthly retention heatmaps.
- [Compare](/docs/compare) — Benchmark performance against historical date ranges across all metrics.
- [Replays](/docs/replays) — Watch session replays to identify broken user experiences and friction points.

## Universal Filtering

Date ranges, custom tags, UTM campaigns, and customer properties can be applied universally across all reports and unit economics dashboards.
